# cprt-rover-arm-2025
Mechanical design and CAD progression for the CPRT 2025 rover arm.

## The Engineering Problem
As a new member of the mechanical subteam I was tasked to come up with a conceptual design that tries to improve upon the previously manufactured rover arm. I focused on the previous' arm issue of instability. The old arm worked using a belt gear system and a differntial gear box as the shoulder joint. Whenever the arm moved it would sway making it inaccurate and unstable. My conceptual idea was to design an arm based off human anatomy where linear actuators are the muscles suporting the frame, focusing on stability and strength.
